# Matchpoint Therapeutics

> Matchpoint Therapeutics is a privately held biotechnology company in Watertown, Massachusetts (Cambridge at launch) discovering precision small-molecule covalent medicines for immune diseases and cancer on its Advanced Covalent Exploration (ACE) platform. Founded by covalent-chemistry, proteomics and computational-science researchers from Stanford University and Harvard Medical School, it closed a $30M seed round co-led by Atlas Venture and Access Biotechnology in November 2021 and launched publicly in October 2022 with $100M total after a $70M Series A led by Sanofi Ventures. In July 2025 it entered an exclusive option and licence agreement with Novartis for oral covalent inhibitors targeting a transcription factor linked to multiple inflammatory diseases.

Matchpoint Therapeutics runs NO developer program. There is no developer portal, no API documentation, no API reference, no SDK, no CLI, no MCP server, no agent card, no sandbox, no pricing and no sign-up. The only machine-readable surface reachable without credentials is the WordPress REST content API at https://matchpointtx.com/wp-json — 222 routes across 13 namespaces, of which the read-only content subset below was verified live on 2026-08-25. It is a content API for a corporate website, not a product API, and Matchpoint does not present it as one.

## Content API (anonymously readable)

Base URL: `https://matchpointtx.com/wp-json` — no authentication, no rate-limit headers, page-number pagination via `page`/`per_page` with `X-WP-Total` and `X-WP-TotalPages`, RFC 8288 `Link` for next/prev. The contract is upstream WordPress, documented at https://developer.wordpress.org/rest-api/.

- `GET /wp/v2/team` — 19 leadership, board, observer and scientific-founder biographies. The only company-specific content type. NOTE: the `team_types` taxonomy is registered but EMPTY, so role grouping is not machine-readable.
- `GET /wp/v2/team/{id}` — one biography.
- `GET /wp/v2/posts` — 5 news posts, October 2022 through July 2025.
- `GET /wp/v2/posts/{id}` — one news post with full rendered body.
- `GET /wp/v2/pages` — 3 pages: homepage, privacy policy, terms of use.
- `GET /wp/v2/pages/{id}` — one page.
- `GET /wp/v2/media` — 43 image attachments (22 PNG, 21 JPEG).
- `GET /wp/v2/search` — cross-content search; 27 objects total.
- `GET /wp/v2/categories` — 1 term (uncategorized). `GET /wp/v2/tags` and `GET /wp/v2/team_types` are registered but empty.
- `GET /wp/v2/comments` — registered, empty.
- `GET /wp/v2/types`, `/taxonomies`, `/statuses`, and `GET /` — discovery documents.
- `GET /oembed/1.0/embed?url=...` — oEmbed 1.0 for matchpointtx.com URLs.

Gated at 401 and deliberately excluded: `/wp/v2/users`, `/wp/v2/settings`, `/wp/v2/menus`, `/wp/v2/menu-items`, `/wp/v2/themes`, `/wp/v2/plugins`, `/wp/v2/templates`, `/wp/v2/template-parts`, `/wp/v2/block-types`, `/wp/v2/icons`, `/wp/v2/font-collections`, `/wp/v2/sidebars`, `/wp/v2/widget-types`, `/oembed/1.0/proxy`, the whole `wp-abilities/v1`, `yoast/v1`, `struck/v1`, `wordfence/v1`, `wpe/cache-plugin/v1`, `wpe_sign_on_plugin/v1`, `wp-site-health/v1` and `wp-block-editor/v1` namespaces, and every write method.
